02 / METHODOLOGY

Every number has a reason.

Base monthly energy = [(households × household use) + (businesses × household use × business multiplier) + (facilities × household use × facility multiplier)] × expected connection uptake.

Scenario range

Low and high values apply the selected sensitivity percentage around the base case. This makes uncertainty visible without pretending it is a statistical confidence interval.

Growth

Year-n demand = base demand × (1 + annual growth rate)ⁿ. Growth is a user assumption, not a learned prediction.

Peak proxy

Monthly energy is converted to average kW and multiplied by a user-selected factor. This cannot replace hourly load profiles or engineering design.

04 / HONEST LIMITATIONS

What this prototype can — and cannot — claim.

✓ CAN

Produce editable demand scenarios from stated assumptions.

✓ CAN

Show how customer mix, uptake and growth affect estimates.

! NOT YET

Claim predictive accuracy or held-out-site validation.

Validation plan

With operator permission, obtain anonymised time-stamped meter readings and site characteristics. Hold out entire sites, compare forecasts against measured demand, and report MAE, RMSE and bias on unseen sites. Until this test is completed, this project reports no validation score.
